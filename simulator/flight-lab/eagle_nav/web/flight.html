WHO DOES WHAT?

The loop that makes it autonomous.

  1. Eagle mission supervisor chooses the next local waypoint and monitors progress. It sends position targets over MAVLink; it does not directly set motor speeds.
  2. ArduPilot EKF3 estimates position and attitude from virtual GPS, inertial, compass, and barometer readings.
  3. ArduPilot controllers turn the target into velocity, tilt, and motor commands. Actual controller output appears beside the map.
  4. SITL quadrotor physics calculates the aircraft response. New simulated sensor readings close the loop.
  5. Mission completion requires returning, landing, and a disarmed heartbeat. A command being sent is not counted as a completed flight.
